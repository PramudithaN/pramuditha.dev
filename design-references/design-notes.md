## 2. Core Concept Notes

### Hero Section: Search-First Experience (Google-Style)
- **Centerpiece:** A prominent, Google-style search bar as the primary focal point of the hero section.
- **Loading Animation:** The search bar must feature a smooth, intentional entrance/loading animation when the page loads.
- **Personal Brand Identity:** Styled and framed with Pramuditha's iconic visual designs so visitors immediately recognize his personal aesthetic and know it's his portfolio/world, while keeping the search bar as the main hero interaction.
- **Pre-Searched Suggestions on Focus:** Selecting/focusing the search bar reveals pre-populated suggested queries (e.g., Projects, About, Gallery, Testimonials, Skills, etc.) so users immediately discover what they can explore.
- **Smart Routing:** Submitting or selecting a query routes users directly to the relevant page or section on the website.
- **Fun Fallback Page (No Match / Off-Site Query):** If a user searches for something that doesn't exist on the website, redirect them to a dedicated **fun interactive playground page** where they can enjoy mini-games, interactive toys, or creative easter eggs instead of a dead-end 404.
